# CBSE Grade 7 Maths Chapters - Simple Student Format

## Chapter 1: Integers
**Explanation:** Integers are whole numbers that can be positive, negative, or zero. This chapter teaches how to add, subtract, multiply, and divide integers.

**Key Points:**
1. Positive numbers are greater than zero (1, 2, 3...)
2. Negative numbers are less than zero (-1, -2, -3...)
3. Addition rule: Same sign → add, keep sign. Different signs → subtract, take larger sign.

**Important Terms:**
1. Integer
2. Positive integer
3. Negative integer
4. Absolute value
5. Additive inverse

**Practice Questions:**
1. Add: (-5) + 8 = ?
2. Subtract: 12 - (-7) = ?
3. Find additive inverse of -9.

## Chapter 2: Fractions and Decimals
**Explanation:** Fractions represent parts of a whole. Decimals are another way to show fractions. Learn to add, subtract, multiply, and divide them.

**Key Points:**
1. Fraction = Numerator/Denominator
2. To add fractions, find common denominator
3. Decimal 0.75 = 3/4

**Important Terms:**
1. Fraction
2. Numerator
3. Denominator
4. Proper fraction
5. Improper fraction

**Practice Questions:**
1. Add: 1/4 + 1/3 = ?
2. Convert 0.5 to fraction.
3. Multiply: 2/3 × 3/4 = ?

## Chapter 3: Data Handling
**Explanation:** Data handling means collecting, organizing, and presenting information using pictographs, bar graphs, and mean/median.

**Key Points:**
1. Pictograph uses pictures to show data
2. Bar graph uses bars of different heights
3. Mean = Total ÷ Number of items

**Important Terms:**
1. Data
2. Pictograph
3. Bar graph
4. Mean
5. Median

**Practice Questions:**
1. Find mean of 2, 4, 6, 8.
2. What is pictograph?
3. Calculate median of 3, 5, 7, 9, 11.

## Chapter 4: Simple Equations
**Explanation:** Equations are mathematical statements with = sign. Learn to solve simple equations like x + 5 = 12.

**Key Points:**
1. Equation: Left = Right
2. To solve, do same operation on both sides
3. Check answer by substituting back

**Important Terms:**
1. Equation
2. Variable
3. Solution
4. Balance
5. Unknown

**Practice Questions:**
1. Solve: x + 3 = 7
2. Solve: y - 4 = 2
3. Solve: 2z = 10

## Chapter 5: Lines and Angles
**Explanation:** Lines can be straight, curved, parallel, or intersecting. Angles are formed when two lines meet.

**Key Points:**
1. Parallel lines never meet
2. Perpendicular lines meet at 90°
3. Adjacent angles share common side

**Important Terms:**
1. Line
2. Angle
3. Parallel lines
4. Perpendicular lines
5. Vertex

**Practice Questions:**
1. What is angle between parallel lines?
2. Name types of angles.
3. Draw perpendicular lines.
