# CBSE Grade 9 Science Chapters - Simple Student Format

## Chapter 1: Matter in Our Surroundings
**Explanation:** Matter has states: solid, liquid, gas. Particles have space/movement. Changes by temperature.

**Key Points:**
1. Solid: Fixed shape/volume
2. Diffusion: Particles mix
3. Evaporation causes cooling

**Important Terms:**
1. Matter
2. Particle
3. Diffusion
4. Evaporation
5. Latent heat

**Practice Questions:**
1. State with fixed shape?
2. Why evaporation cools?
3. Arrange particles: gas > liquid > solid?

## Chapter 2: Is Matter Around Us Pure?
**Explanation:** Mixtures vs compounds. Solutions, suspensions, colloids, separation techniques.

**Key Points:**
1. Pure substance: Element/compound
2. Solution: Solute + solvent
3. Centrifugation for suspensions

**Important Terms:**
1. Mixture
2. Solution
3. Colloid
4. Suspension
5. Solute

**Practice Questions:**
1. Example of colloid?
2. Separate cream from milk?
3. Solution or mixture: air?

## Chapter 3: Atoms and Molecules
**Explanation:** Atoms are building blocks. Molecules, ions, atomic/molecular mass, laws of conservation.

**Key Points:**
1. Atom: Smallest particle
2. Molecule: Group of atoms
3. Atomicity: Number of atoms/molecule

**Important Terms:**
1. Atom
2. Molecule
3. Atomic mass
4. Mole
5. Ion

**Practice Questions:**
1. Molecular mass H2O?
2. Law of constant proportions?
3. What is diatomic?

## Chapter 4: Structure of the Atom
**Explanation:** Atom structure: electrons, protons, neutrons. Valency, atomic models.

**Key Points:**
1. Nucleus: Protons + neutrons
2. Electrons in shells
3. Atomic number = protons

**Important Terms:**
1. Electron
2. Proton
3. Neutron
4. Nucleus
5. Valency

**Practice Questions:**
1. Atomic number of Carbon?
2. Location of protons?
3. Shells for electrons?

## Chapter 5: Motion
**Explanation:** Describing motion: distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration, equations.

**Key Points:**
1. Speed = distance/time
2. Acceleration = velocity change/time
3. Uniform/non-uniform motion

**Important Terms:**
1. Motion
2. Displacement
3. Speed
4. Velocity
5. Acceleration

**Practice Questions:**
1. Speed if 100m in 20s?
2. Difference speed/velocity?
3. Equation v = u + at meaning?
