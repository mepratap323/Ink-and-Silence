# CBSE Grade 7 Science Chapters - Simple Student Format

## Chapter 1: Nutrition in Plants
**Explanation:** Plants make their own food through photosynthesis using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. This chapter explains how plants get nutrients.

**Key Points:**
1. Autotrophic nutrition: Plants make food themselves
2. Photosynthesis: Process using chlorophyll, sunlight
3. Nutrients from soil reach plants through roots

**Important Terms:**
1. Photosynthesis
2. Chlorophyll
3. Autotroph
4. Heterotroph
5. Stomata

**Practice Questions:**
1. What gas plants take in during photosynthesis?
2. Name the green pigment in leaves.
3. How do plants get water?

## Chapter 2: Nutrition in Animals
**Explanation:** Animals cannot make food, so they eat plants or other animals. Learn about human digestive system and different feeding habits.

**Key Points:**
1. Holozoic nutrition: Eating solid food
2. Human digestion: Mouth → Stomach → Intestines
3. Amoeba uses pseudopodia to eat

**Important Terms:**
1. Digestion
2. Herbivores
3. Carnivores
4. Omnivores
5. Ruminants

**Practice Questions:**
1. Name 3 parts of human digestive system.
2. What do herbivores eat?
3. How does amoeba feed?

## Chapter 3: Heat
**Explanation:** Heat is a form of energy. Learn how heat transfers by conduction, convection, radiation, and effects of heat on objects.

**Key Points:**
1. Conduction: Heat through solids
2. Convection: Heat through liquids/gases
3. Radiation: Heat through space

**Important Terms:**
1. Heat
2. Temperature
3. Conductor
4. Insulator
5. Thermometer

**Practice Questions:**
1. Which metal conducts heat best?
2. Explain convection with example.
3. What measures temperature?

## Chapter 4: Acids, Bases and Salts
**Explanation:** Acids taste sour, bases taste bitter. Learn their properties, pH scale, and neutralization reaction.

**Key Points:**
1. pH < 7: Acid, pH > 7: Base
2. Indicators: Litmus turns red in acid
3. Acid + Base → Salt + Water

**Important Terms:**
1. Acid
2. Base
3. pH scale
4. Neutralization
5. Indicator

**Practice Questions:**
1. Litmus is blue in which substance?
2. pH of pure water?
3. Product of neutralization?

## Chapter 5: Physical and Chemical Changes
**Explanation:** Physical changes don't make new substances (melting), chemical changes do (rusting). Learn to identify them.

**Key Points:**
1. Physical: Shape changes, reversible
2. Chemical: New substance, irreversible usually
3. Rusting is chemical change

**Important Terms:**
1. Physical change
2. Chemical change
3. Reversible
4. Irreversible
5. Rusting

**Practice Questions:**
1. Melting ice is what change?
2. Example of chemical change?
3. Burning paper is reversible?
