Grade 9 Mathematics

Chapter 1: Number Systems

Explanation: Real numbers include rational and irrational numbers. Learn properties of numbers, decimal expansions.

Key Points:

  1. Rational: p/q form (terminating/repeating decimal)
  2. Irrational: Non-terminating, non-repeating (√2, π)
  3. Real numbers = Rational + Irrational

Important Terms:

Rational number
Number expressible as fraction p/q (q≠0)
Irrational number
Cannot be expressed as fraction (non-repeating decimal)
Real number
All numbers on number line
Decimal expansion
Writing number in decimal form
Irrationality proof
Method to show number is irrational

Practice Questions:

  1. Is 0.333... rational? Why?
  2. Decimal expansion of 1/8?
  3. Prove √2 irrational (basic steps).

Chapter 2: Polynomials

Explanation: Polynomials are expressions like 2x² + 3x - 1. Learn zeros, factorisation, division.

Key Points:

  1. Degree: Highest power
  2. Zero of polynomial p(a) = 0
  3. Factor theorem: p(a)=0 → (x-a) factor

Important Terms:

Polynomial
Expression ax^n + bx^{n-1} + ...
Zero/Root
Value making p(x)=0
Factorisation
Writing as product of factors
Degree
Highest power of variable
Coefficient
Number multiplying variable

Practice Questions:

  1. Zeros of x² - 5x + 6 = 0?
  2. Factor x² + 5x + 6
  3. Degree of 3x³ + 2x - 1?

Chapter 3: Coordinate Geometry

Explanation: Cartesian plane with x,y axes. Plot points, distance, section formula.

Key Points:

  1. Quadrants: I(+ +), II(- +), III(- -), IV(+ -)
  2. Distance formula: √[(x2-x1)² + (y2-y1)²]
  3. Section formula for dividing line

Important Terms:

Cartesian plane
Plane with x,y axes
Abscissa
x-coordinate
Ordinate
y-coordinate
Quadrant
4 regions divided by axes
Distance formula
Distance between two points

Practice Questions:

  1. Distance from (0,0) to (3,4)?
  2. Point dividing (1,2),(4,6) in 1:1 ratio?
  3. Which quadrant x>0, y<0?

Chapter 4: Linear Equations in Two Variables

Explanation: Equations like 2x + 3y = 6. Graph lines, solve systems.

Key Points:

  1. General form ax + by + c = 0
  2. Parallel lines: same slope
  3. Consistent system: unique solution

Important Terms:

Linear equation
Equation of degree 1
Simultaneous equations
Solve two equations together
Graph of line
Straight line on Cartesian plane
Slope
Steepness of line
Intercept
Where line crosses axis

Practice Questions:

  1. Graph 2x + y = 4
  2. Solve: x + y = 5, x - y = 1
  3. Line parallel to y = 2x + 1?

Chapter 5: Introduction to Euclid's Geometry

Explanation: Euclid's axioms, postulates for plane geometry. Equivalent versions.

Key Points:

  1. Axiom: Universal truth
  2. Postulate: Geometry-specific
  3. Two distinct lines intersect at most one point

Important Terms:

Axiom
Obvious truth accepted without proof
Postulate
Axiom specific to geometry
Euclid
Father of geometry
Undefined terms
Point, line, plane
Proof
Logical argument using axioms

Practice Questions:

  1. Difference between axiom and postulate?
  2. How many points determine a line?
  3. Example of axiom from chapter.
