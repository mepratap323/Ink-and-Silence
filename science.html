Grade 9 Science

Chapter 1: Matter in Our Surroundings

Explanation: Matter has states: solid, liquid, gas. Particles have space/movement. Changes by temperature.

Key Points:

  1. Solid: Fixed shape/volume
  2. Diffusion: Particles mix
  3. Evaporation causes cooling

Important Terms:

Matter
Anything that has mass/volume
Particle
Tiny unit of matter
Diffusion
Mixing of particles
Evaporation
Liquid to gas slowly
Latent heat
Heat for state change

Practice Questions:

  1. State of matter with fixed shape?
  2. Why evaporation cools things?
  3. Arrange particle spacing: gas > liquid > solid?

Chapter 2: Is Matter Around Us Pure?

Explanation: Mixtures vs compounds. Solutions, suspensions, colloids, separation techniques.

Key Points:

  1. Pure substance: Element/compound
  2. Solution: Solute + solvent
  3. Centrifugation for suspensions

Important Terms:

Mixture
Two or more substances mixed
Solution
Homogeneous mixture
Colloid
Mixture appearing homogeneous but not
Suspension
Heterogeneous, particles settle
Solute
Substance dissolved

Practice Questions:

  1. Example of colloid?
  2. How to separate cream from milk?
  3. Is air solution or mixture?

Chapter 3: Atoms and Molecules

Explanation: Atoms are building blocks. Molecules, ions, atomic/molecular mass, laws of conservation.

Key Points:

  1. Atom: Smallest particle
  2. Molecule: Group of atoms
  3. Atomicity: Number of atoms/molecule

Important Terms:

Atom
Basic unit of element
Molecule
Group of atoms bonded
Atomic mass
Mass of atom
Mole
6.022×10²³ particles
Ion
Charged atom

Practice Questions:

  1. Molecular mass of H₂O?
  2. Law of constant proportions?
  3. What is diatomic molecule?

Displacement

Speed

Velocity

Acceleration
